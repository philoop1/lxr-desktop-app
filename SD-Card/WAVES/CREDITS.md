# Wavetable credits

The wavetables in `WAVES.BIN` (and the older `*.OLD` versions) were converted
for the LXR-02 with `tools/make_wavetables.py` from the free sampler of
**KRC Mathwaves** by Kevin R. Crosley:

- Free collection: https://www.wavetables.lol/l/free
- Full collection: https://kcrosley.gumroad.com/l/wavetables

All rights to the original waveforms stay with their author. They are not
covered by the firmware licence of this repository. If you like them, get the
originals from the links above and support the author.
